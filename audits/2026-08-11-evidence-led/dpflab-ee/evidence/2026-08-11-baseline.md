## Confirmed technical defect

Production emits `<script type="application/ld+json">{structuredData}</script>` literally. This is invalid JSON-LD and must be fixed and retested before structured data can be treated as implemented. The visible business claims encoded by the intended schema remain subject to the claim ledger.

## Measurement/configuration boundary

- The repository has persisted website leads, CRM milestones, Meta Instant Form ingestion, retryable delivery outbox, Meta/Google event adapters and daily marketing metrics.
- Develop has named Meta/CRM/notification secrets except `META_APP_SECRET`. Callback verification was observed end to end (`200`, exact challenge echoed, `Cache-Control: no-store`), but an unsigned POST returns `503 integration_unavailable`; lead ingestion therefore remains unavailable until the matching Meta App Secret is installed.
- `META_CRM_EVENTS_ENABLED`, `META_SITE_CRM_EVENTS_ENABLED` and `GOOGLE_CRM_EVENTS_ENABLED` are false in develop. This is a safe prepared state, not a verified provider integration.
- The separate develop cron Worker is deployed with its own `CRM_CRON_TOKEN` and calls the main Worker through a service binding every 30 minutes. This establishes scheduling presence, not provider-delivery correctness.
- No end-to-end real-lead, duplicate, retry, payment or provider-feedback scenario was executed in this capture. Technical readiness must not be reported as attribution or ROI proof.
