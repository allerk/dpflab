# Audience and decision contexts

The current business view suggests three relevant contexts: an owner/operator with a DPF/FAP problem; a workshop or partner handling removal/installation; and a non-Russian/non-Estonian visitor who may need English. These are business hypotheses, not validated personas or market sizes.

## Evidence and access limits

- Available: owner/delegated-operator conversation, current public form choices, implemented CRM route fields and current multilingual site.
- Missing: sampled lead conversations, CRM outcomes, call reasons, loss reasons at usable volume, Search Console query/page exports, market/language splits, partner interviews and paid-ad audience performance joined to paid jobs.
- Search volume or Meta platform leads must not be used as population size or sales evidence.
- The English variant may be operationally useful, but no current evidence proves English demand or a separate English acquisition strategy.

## Research route

Use the DPFLAB service blueprint first because website qualification depends on offline diagnosis, filter location, partner removal/installation, transport, cleaning and return. Use search-intent ownership only after current query/page and ad-destination evidence is available. Do not invent demographic personas.
